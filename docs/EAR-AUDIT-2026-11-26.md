## What is wrong in the design (not only bugs)

1. **Gain stacking.** PGA 20 dB on the ADC, then `MIC_GAIN = 32` (+30 dB) in software, then every
   phrase is peak-normalised before STT and voice ID. The room noise is lifted as much as the voice;
   loud moments clip after the ×32 and the AEC diverges (resets) on clipped input.
2. **No noise suppression at all.** Only a 120 Hz high-pass and the AEC. Fans, canaries, TV, kids
   all reach the VAD, STT and voice ID as they are.
3. **One VAD decides everything on the weakest device.** Silero on the Station, on the AEC output:
   barge-in over music depends on the AEC's residual; impulses (a stamp) pass; a stuck state is
   deaf for minutes. Nothing on the hub double-checks.
4. **No measurement loop.** Nothing records "what the ear heard vs what was said" to tune against;
   every threshold was set from a few cases.

## Plan

**Step 1 - measure (needs you, ~15 min).** The ear dumps its raw 7 mics, the reference and the
processed signal (EVA_EAR_DUMP, already in ear.py) while you do a short script:
- 20 s of silence; 20 s of the room as it is (canaries, TV);
- ordinary phrases from 1 m, 3 m and the far end of the room, by you and by Таня;
- a stamp, a door, a clap;
- music at the usual level: «Ева, стоп», «Ева, тише», «стоп» from 1 m and 3 m.
Offline then: SNR per stage, where clipping starts, the AEC's ERLE and why it resets, Silero's
probabilities on raw / AEC / AEC+denoise, speaker-ID similarity per stage.

**Step 2 - fix the chain from the numbers.** Expected direction (to be confirmed by step 1):
- gain: a lower fixed software gain or a slow AGC instead of ×32 + peak normalisation;
- noise suppression: RNNoise on the Station if it fits its CPU (the TV page takes ~77 %), else
  DeepFilterNet on the hub before STT and voice ID (and the VAD decision double-checked there);
- AEC: no adaptation on clipped blocks, a longer filter if the room needs it;
- barge-in over music: her name / «стоп» from a small keyword model on the AEC residual, or
  ducking the music on any voice-like sound for a moment;
- the stuck-VAD guard also over music.

**Step 3 - voice prints again** on the fixed chain: re-enrol each family voice (a few phrases each,
clean), drop prints recorded at 36 dB, rebuild guest groups from scratch.

**Step 4 - a regression set**: the step-1 recordings become a replay test (tools/ear-replay),
run before every ear change.

## 2026-11-26 evening: the user's rule, the hardware, ready-made parts

**Rule (user):** the Station is hardware only. Whatever the chips do themselves (PGA, the ADC's
own AGC or filters) is fine; once the samples are digital the Station does nothing more with them.
No echo canceller, no VAD, no cutting on its A53 - all of it on shn-linux.

**Found in the logs:** «стоп» over music never reached the brain because the Station's own VAD
gave 0.00 for whole songs - in all three music sessions of the day (11:16, 11:47, 18:26, each put
on by a listener; the brain path is the same as a voice command's). So the failing part is exactly
the processing on the Station.

**The hardware (hackalice.me/devices/station-gen1, MyszkinPL docs PR, Habr 2018, TI):**
- 7 mics (1 centre + 6 on a circle) on 5x TLV320ADC3101 in TDM, plus **3 reference taps** of the
  amplifiers (2 on the bus-0 ADC, 1 on bus 1). We use one (word 4 high); the other two are in our
  capture (word 0) and unused. 2x TAS5751M: a woofer (mono) and tweeters (stereo); at high volume
  the vendor switches to stereo - one tap does not see all of what plays.
- TLV320ADC3101: PGA 0-40 dB in 0.5 dB; a per-channel hardware AGC (target, attack/decay,
  noise gate, max gain), exposed by the mainline driver as ALSA controls; a miniDSP for IIR/FIR
  filters only (no noise suppression, no beamforming). A hardware AGC before an echo canceller
  makes the echo path pump, so it stays off on the mics; a fixed PGA is right.
- The vendor firmware did DOA -> beamforming -> noise suppression -> dereverberation -> AEC on the
  SoC (quasar). Yandex's AEC (Habr 2023): STFT + a Kalman filter per frequency bin; their lesson -
  keep the reference in the mics' clock domain (we have it: the taps are on the same TDM bus).

**Ready-made instead of our own:** WebRTC's audio processing module (AEC3 + noise suppression +
high-pass + AGC2, multichannel reference) - the canceller of Chrome and PipeWire. Python wheels
for x86_64: `livekit` (`rtc.AudioProcessingModule`, 10 ms frames, `process_reverse_stream`) or
`aec-audio-processing`. On the hub it costs nothing.

**Plan:** the Station streams its raw capture (5 x S32 at 16 kHz = 320 KB/s, the music/speaking
flags multiplexed per block) to the hub over one TCP connection; the hub runs WebRTC APM (AEC3 with
the taps as the reference, NS, HPF) -> Silero VAD -> the same cutting rules -> hear.py in-process.
The LED / "I hear you" events go from the hub to the Station's UI. Then the measurements of step 1
are made on the hub from the stream, no special session needed.

**Done 2026-11-26 19:25 (47727a4):** app/ear.py on the Station is now only arecord -> one TCP
connection (6 % of its CPU); services/stt/ear_stream.py on the hub does high-pass -> WebRTC APM
(livekit 1.3.0: AEC3 + noise suppression + its high-pass, AGC off) -> Silero -> the old cutting
rules -> hear.STATION in process (16 % of one hub core). Measured on a raw capture with the TV on:
her voice suppressed by 11-18 dB where our NLMS took 1-6 dB; live, one 8 s phrase of hers left
0.7 s of echo (dropped), "aec out/in -35 dB" over the minute. The black box: `POST :8901/hear/box`

**The TV, 2026-11-26 evening (19bba2a, 77f7b1c).** The admin's «Места» page gives each marked place a
mode: mute / listen only from there (`place_opts` in stt-keep/admin-settings.json). Two layers:
- logic (services/stt/hear.py `muted_place`): a phrase from a muted place (or outside the "only"
  places) is marked, teaches the voices nothing, and the brain drops it unless «Ева» is said by a
  known family voice;
- sound (services/stt/ear_null.py): a fixed STFT beamformer over the 7 raw mics, made from the STT
  log's -mics.wav clips (covariance of the muted places' blocks vs family speech from elsewhere,
  generalised eigenvector, fitted to the old mono; fades to the plain mono below 150-500 Hz). It
  sits before AEC3 (linear, fixed: one echo path); the reference and raw mics are delayed by its
  256 samples. Remade automatically when the muted places change. TV only: -12.7 dB, speech
  elsewhere -1.6 dB; with the bedroom/closet/toilet muted too: -9.4 / -2.1 dB. On the 19:18
  capture the TV came 9-10 dB lower, but Silero still calls it speech at any level - the logical
  mute is what drops its phrases; the null makes a person over the TV ~10 dB cleaner.
